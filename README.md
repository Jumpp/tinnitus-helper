# Frequency Therapy

A single-file web app for high-frequency hearing rehabilitation. Plays targeted frequencies per ear in the 6 kHz–12 kHz range, designed to stimulate hearing at just below the audible threshold.

No install, no build step, no dependencies.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the hosted version: https://jumpp.github.io/tinnitus-helper

Works on mobile. Use headphones.

## Calibration

1. Start a session
2. For each frequency band, raise the L and R levels until you can just barely hear the tone
3. lower the level until it disappears — that's your target
4. Repeat per ear independently

**Volume fine-tune** compensates for device volume. If your phone or computer is louder than usual, lower it. If quieter, raise it. Recalibrate after adjusting, since the threshold shifts with output level.

## During a session

If a frequency becomes audible, you should recalibrate — step the level back down until it disappears. The goal is always just below your hearing threshold

## Session length

One hour per day is a good starting point. The timer stops the session automatically. You can do more, but give your ears time to rest between sessions.
