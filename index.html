Frequency Therapy

How to use
Calibration For each frequency, raise the L and R levels until you can just barely hear the tone, then step back one until it disappears. That’s your target — just below audible. Do this for each ear independently. Volume fine-tune Compensates for system volume. If your device is louder than usual, lower this. If quieter, raise it. Recalibrate after changing it, since the threshold shifts. During a session If a frequency becomes clearly audible, recalibrate — step it back down until it disappears again. The goal is always just below your hearing threshold. Session length One hour per day is a good starting point. The timer stops automatically. You can do more, but give your ears time to rest.
